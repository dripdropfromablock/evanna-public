Sideways Now Playing, Georgia style

After the foobar2000 Georgia-ReBORN theme, redrawn the iOS way: a segmented control on top, frosted grouped rows, a glass control bar, and the blurred cover behind everything. Tap Lyrics in the control or the bar and the disc rolls back into the cover, the cover slides left, and the words come in. Codec, channel and disc pictures are the theme's own (MIT licence). The green "Live" phone loops it (open this file in a browser).
